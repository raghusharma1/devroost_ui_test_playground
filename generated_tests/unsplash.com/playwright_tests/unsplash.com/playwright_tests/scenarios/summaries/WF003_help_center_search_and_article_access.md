## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://help.unsplash.com/en/
2. Type 'license' into the 'Search for articles...' input field and press Enter
3. Click on the 'Unsplash License' collection from the results
4. Click on the article 'Can I use Unsplash images for personal or commercial projects?'
5. Verify the article body text and 'Written by' metadata are visible.
