## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://help.unsplash.com/en/
2. Type 'license' into the 'Search for articles...' input field and press Enter.
3. Click on the article titled 'Unsplash License'.
4. Verify the article content loads, then click the 'Help Center' breadcrumb to return to the home page.
