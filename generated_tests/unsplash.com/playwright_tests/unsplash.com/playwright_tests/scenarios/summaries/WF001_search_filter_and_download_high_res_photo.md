## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://unsplash.com
2. Type 'nature' into the main search bar and press Enter.
3. Click the 'Filters' button or the 'Orientation' dropdown and select 'Landscape'.
4. Click on any photo thumbnail in the results grid to open the detail view.
5. Click the 'Download free' button in the top right corner of the modal.
6. Verify that the 'Say thanks' attribution pop-up appears.
