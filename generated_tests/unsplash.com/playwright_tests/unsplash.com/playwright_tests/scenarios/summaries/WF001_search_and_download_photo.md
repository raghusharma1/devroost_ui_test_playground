## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://unsplash.com
2. Click into the search bar at the top (placeholder 'Search high-resolution images')
3. Type 'mountains' and press Enter
4. Click on the first non-sponsored photo in the results grid to open the detail modal
5. Click the 'Download free' button in the top right corner of the modal
6. Verify that the image download starts and a 'Say thanks' attribution modal appears.
